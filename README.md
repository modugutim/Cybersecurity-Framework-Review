## Objective

Give executives a clear recommendation on which frameworks to adopt, in what order, and why, based on the organization's regulatory exposure, risk appetite and maturity.

## Execution

- I compared ISO/IEC 27001, NIST CSF 2.0 and CIS Controls v8 on purpose, scope, certification, adoption effort and best-fit use.
- I separated governance use cases from operational ones, showing how the three layer together instead of competing.
- I built a phased adoption model and presented it to the business with the cost, effort and assurance trade-offs of each path.

## Business outcomes

- Provided leadership with a strategic roadmap for framework adoption, optimizing resource allocation by eliminating redundant controls across frameworks.
- Defined a maturity path with milestones so leadership can track progress.

## Framework comparison

| Strategy aspect | ISO/IEC 27001 | NIST CSF 2.0 | CIS Controls v8 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Primary purpose** | Certifiable ISMS | Strategic risk management | Practical security safeguards |
| **Orientation** | Process and policy driven | Outcome and lifecycle based | Implementation and technical driven |
| **Strategic scope** | Enterprise governance | Cyber risk lifecycle | Technical and operational safeguards |
| **Certification** | Yes | No | No |
| **Primary utility** | Compliance and contractual audit | Strategic security alignment | Tactical defense and baselines |

## Skills demonstrated

- Framework comparison and selection
- Executive communication
- Governance vs. operational control design
- Maturity roadmapping
